ROOM FOR REAL LIFE

Make room for
the days you actually have.

01

Pick one thing.

Reading, walking, journaling. Choose something you want to do and decide when it fits into your day.

02

Give busy days an option.

Set a smaller goal in advance. Ten minutes when you can, one sentence when you can’t. Record either.

03

Adjust as you go.

Change a plan that feels too much. When you no longer need daily check-ins, stop tracking and keep your history.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few things to know.

Do I have to do the full goal every day?

No. Choose an easier option for busy days and record it when that is what you can do.

What happens if I stop tracking?

Your history stays. You can start tracking again when you want to.

Is my data synced between devices?

No automatic sync is provided. Plus includes backup and restore. Purchase processing uses RevenueCat and the platform store.
