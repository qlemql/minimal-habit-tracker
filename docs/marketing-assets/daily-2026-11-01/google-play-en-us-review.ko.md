## 검토 가능한 수정안 — 미반영

Title (유지):

Ssak: Habit Tracker

Short description:

Track habits with a usual plan and a smaller option for busy days.

Full description:

Ssak is a habit tracker for the days your usual plan does not fit. Set two options in advance: read for ten minutes on a usual day, or read one page on a busy day. At check-in, record the option you actually did. One page is not recorded as ten minutes.

MAKE A PLAN THAT FITS YOUR LIFE
Choose a habit, when you will do it, and a smaller alternative for busy days. Use it for reading, walking, journaling, or another action you want to practice. Set a weekly goal that leaves room for days off.

RECORD WHAT YOU DID
Choose your usual action or your smaller alternative. Review your weekly history without treating a small action as the full goal. Ssak records the option you choose; it does not measure exact reading time or page counts automatically.

REVIEW AND ADJUST
Review last week's progress, including your usual actions and smaller alternatives. Keep your plan or adjust your smaller alternative, when you will act, and your weekly goal. Past records remain unchanged.

KEEP YOUR HISTORY
When you no longer need to track a habit, finish tracking it and make room for something new. Your past records remain available to revisit.

ONE HABIT IS FREE
Track one habit at a time with no time limit. No ads, subscription, or account required.

OPTIONAL SSAK PLUS
A one-time in-app purchase lets you track up to three habits at once and export or import backup files.

YOUR RECORDS AND PURCHASES
Habit records stay on your device and do not sync automatically between devices. With Ssak Plus, you can save and move records using backup files. Importing a backup replaces existing records, so review the backup information before restoring.

Purchases can be restored using the same store account. Android and iOS purchases are separate.
